# 06 — Bidirectional beam with a wide meet-in-the-middle join

## Hypothesis

Two shorter beams may retain complementary halves even when a one-direction beam loses the complete trajectory.

## Intervention

Build independent direct and reverse beams, retain all configured depths, map each complete reverse frontier into direct coordinates, hash-index every row, then confirm meetings by exact state equality. No known midpoint or protected path is supplied.

Joined candidates are reconstructed, transformed to original coordinates, and replayed exactly.
